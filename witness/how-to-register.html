Registering with Bakobo's witnesses

Registration is how a controller becomes a customer of Bakobo's production witnesses. The witnesses accept events without it, but the production terms promise nothing to unregistered use.

How to register

Write to info@bakobo.com, and include:

  • an Ed25519 public key that you control, which Bakobo will use to recognize requests from you;
  • a sentence or two about what you intend to witness;
  • how you would like to be contacted.

Bakobo will reply to arrange the rest. Registration is handled by hand for now, so expect a person rather than an automated answer.

Send only the public key. Never send a private key, a seed, or a passcode. Nobody at Bakobo will ever ask for one.
