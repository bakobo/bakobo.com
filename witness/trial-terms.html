Terms for Bakobo's trial witness

The trial witness exists so that people can try witnessing without asking anyone first. It is free, and it is deliberately not backed by any promise.

  • There is no service-level agreement and no availability guarantee. Nothing monitors the host, and it may be unavailable at any time without notice.
  • Bakobo may delete the witness's data at any time, without notice. No backups are taken, so anything deleted or lost is gone.
  • A rebuilt trial witness comes back under a new identifier. A witness that returned under its old key with a shortened history could sign receipts for a contradictory one, so a rebuild cannot keep the old identity.
  • An identifier witnessed by the trial witness must never be used to establish a real-world relationship: not an identity anyone relies on, not a credential, not an agreement. If something real depends on it, use a witness that carries a commitment.

Bakobo's production witnesses are separate, and their terms are published separately. Questions go to info@bakobo.com.
